## 2. Project summary
BLACKOUT is a Meta Quest 2 VR experience. A city-wide power cut leaves a market city pitch dark. The player sends sonar pings that reveal the streets as glowing echoes, follows three relay tones through the night market and sends a voice message at the radio tower. When it is delivered the city relights slowly. The experience is built around sound, comfort and accessibility.

## 5. Accessibility and safety
- Health and comfort notice before play; reduce flashing on by default
- Sonar cooldown 1.2 seconds, smooth light pulse (dimmed with Reduce Flashing), relight over 3.5 to 5 seconds, no flashing content
- Captions for every spoken line and important sound with adjustable size, high-contrast mode
- Each cue has a sound, a visual and a caption, so no single sense is required
- Seated mode, one-handed option, snap or smooth turn, gaze select, brightness and volume controls

## 6. Technical overview
- Unity 6000.4.6f1, URP 17.4, XR Interaction Toolkit 3.4.1, OpenXR with Meta Quest support, Android ARM64, IL2CPP, Vulkan
- Sonar rays are cast at ping time and echoes are released as the wave reaches each hit point, drawn by a single particle system
- Only eight real-time lights follow the player; every lamp and sign shows an emissive glow
- Static batching, shared materials and no real-time shadows keep the city within Quest 2 limits
- All audio is original, generated by script

## 7. Tests and verification
- 16 automated EditMode tests (settings, captions, game flow, UI contrast and size, sonar cooldown, objective tuning, gradual relight)
- Play-mode run-through of menu, ping, relay and delivery in the Unity Editor
- Quest 2 APK builds as valid ARM64 with the OpenXR loader
- On-headset results recorded with `Docs/Headset-Test-Sheet.md`

## 8. Challenges and reflection
Disk space, a quick-tap UI bug, flat early lighting and square echoes all taught us to test early and to keep effects cheap. Making every cue available in three forms (sound, visual, caption) also made the game clearer for everyone.
